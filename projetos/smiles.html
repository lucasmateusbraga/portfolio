Project 03 · Loyalty and subscriptions

Clube Smiles+, from the offer to the first stream.

Smiles+ adds streaming services to Clube Smiles, the miles subscription from GOL. I designed the journey from the first proposal with the business team to plan choice, checkout, activation and the monthly switch between services.

RoleSenior Product Designer
ScopeOffer, checkout, activation, design system
PlatformWeb, desktop and mobile
LaunchJune 15, 2026
smiles.com.br/clube-smiles-plus

The Clube Smiles+ sales page, with Disney+, Max, NBA League Pass and PlayKids+ as partners.

The challenge

Customers already paid for Clube Smiles. Smiles+ had to feel like more value on top of that, and each streaming partner had its own rules about brand, price and content.

The solution

A journey that explains the add-on first, compares plans with the same structure, reuses the customer's Smiles data and keeps activation inside the flow.

My role

Product definition with the PM and Clube leadership, reviews with the partners, UI, components in the Tangerina design system and the handoff to Engineering.

Context

Smiles+ is an add-on to Clube Smiles. The page had to make that obvious.

Clube Smiles already combined a monthly payment, miles and travel benefits. Smiles+ adds streaming from external partners without replacing the customer's current plan. The first thing the experience had to answer was simple: what do I already have, what am I adding and how much more will I pay?

Constraints

Many people had a say in how the offer could look.

Meli+, from Mercado Livre, was one of the references the business team brought: a subscription that adds entertainment from other companies. Smiles had more rules underneath, and the prototypes became the place where everyone reviewed them together.

Streaming partners

Reviewed the prototypes and set rules for logos, banners, descriptions and how their service could be compared.

Clube Smiles and Product

Eligibility, plan logic, recurring billing and what the add-on could promise.

Marketing and Content

An attractive offer within the partner rules, and clear words for billing and activation.

Tangerina design system

GOL and Smiles were moving to Tangerina, and some Smiles+ needs had no component yet.

Engineering

Feasibility, component behaviour, tokens and the details of the handoff.

Key decisionSell the combined value of Clube, miles and streaming, without putting prices side by side with the partners.

Sales page

The page follows the order of the customer's questions.

What is it, what do I get on top of Clube, which plan fits me, which services are included, how do I activate them and what if I have a doubt. Each block answers one of these, in this order.

  1. What is it
  2. What I add
  3. Which plan
  4. Which services
  5. How to activate
  6. Questions

Plan cards

The plan card changed when the offer became two plans.

The first card was built for a single offer. With two plans, price, number of services and partners stopped lining up, and comparing got hard. I rebuilt the card so both plans show the same information in the same order: name, benefits, services, price and button.

Try it

Selection is confirmed by more than colour

Choose a plan. The border, the label and the button change together, and the button copy says what happens next.

Checkout

Customers confirm their data instead of typing it again.

Smiles already had the email and phone. The streaming partners needed them to create access, so the form comes prefilled. A line next to the fields says that confirming them here does not change the Smiles account.

After purchase

Paying is not the end. The customer still has to start watching.

After payment, the flow shows how to activate each service in three steps. And because customers can switch services once a month, the interface always says which one is active and whether a switch is still available.

Try it

One switch a month

Pick another service. After the switch, the button locks and the card tells the customer when the next switch will be available.

Design system

Built on Tangerina while GOL and Smiles were adopting it.

For each need, I checked what Tangerina already covered. Where it did, I reused it. Where the context was different, I proposed a variant. Where nothing existed, like the plan card states and the monthly switch, I took the new pattern to the design system team and Engineering.

01Reuse

Type, spacing, surfaces and existing patterns.

02Adapt

Variants for the Smiles+ context.

03Create

Plan and partner states that did not exist yet.

04Share

New components reviewed with the design system team and Engineering.

Delivery

Every state in the handoff had a trigger and a next step.

In Figma, the main flow ran horizontally and the variations of each screen sat below it, so Design, Engineering and QA could see the whole journey and every alternative state in one place. These were the questions each area had to answer before building.

Reflection

The customer should not have to understand the rules behind the offer.

Smiles+ had rules from Clube, from four partners, from billing and from a design system in transition. My job was to turn those into a sequence of simple choices. It was also a project where I followed the product from the business proposal to the handoff, with partners in the room.
