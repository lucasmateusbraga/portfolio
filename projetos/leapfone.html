Project 05 · Corporate website

Leapfone: institutional redesign

Redesigning Leapfone's B2B/B2C website to communicate the company's maturity and capacity to scale, right as it prepared to raise a funding round.

Role Product Designer, end to end
Collaboration Marketing, Growth
Scope Research, UX writing, SEO
Platform Website

Section

Context

A website that had to grow up fast.

Leapfone was preparing to open a funding round with investors, and the institutional website suddenly had an essential role in that moment: communicating the product's maturity, its ability to scale, and reinforcing the clarity and credibility of the service.

I led a deep research effort into the business itself, mapping how the company communicated with users, its positioning against competitors, and the opportunities to evolve the digital experience.

The problem: the existing site was written for individual customers renting a phone for themselves, when the actual audience walking into a funding conversation was investors and B2B decision makers evaluating whether this was a serious, scalable business.

Section

Funnel analysis

The funnel was simple. Finding the button was not.

I identified friction points across the funnel: visitor lands, tries to understand the service, and only then requests a proposal. The old site presented content that was hard to scan, with CTAs buried below decorative blocks.

Solution: reposition CTAs above the fold, build a clear path to 'Request a proposal', and organize information progressively: value proposition, benefits, social proof, comparison and action.

Section

Data and hypotheses

Letting the data point at what actually hurt.

To guide the redesign, I used a data-driven UX approach: traffic in GA4, heatmaps and session recordings in Clarity, and dashboards in Looker Studio tracked in real time with the marketing and growth team.

The data showed three clear points to improve: the main CTA was hidden on mobile, the form was too long and caused abandonment, and the content felt generic, without conveying trust. From there, I formulated hypotheses and redesigned with a focus on clarity, credibility and less friction.

GA4 Clarity Looker Studio

Section

SEO and content strategy

Writing the way the client speaks, not the way the product is built.

I worked with the marketing team to align on B2B keywords, such as 'centralized device management', 'corporate smartphones' and 'device rental for companies'. I rewrote microcopy and headers based on UX writing principles and customer research, using the real language clients used day to day, like 'cash flow', 'up to date equipment' and 'fast replacement'.

We also improved the semantic structure (H1, H2, meta tags) for SEO, which helped the content become easier to scan for people and search engines alike.

Results

A site ready for an investor conversation, not just a customer one.

The end result is a more direct institutional website, with clear visual hierarchy and messaging that reflects the company's current stage: more solid, strategic and ready to grow.

What this project reinforced for me is that a website redesign is rarely just a design problem, it is an audience problem first. Once we agreed on who the site was actually talking to, most layout and copy decisions became obvious.

  • 3 hypotheses formulated from real behavioral data (GA4 + Clarity)
  • 1 funnel restructured, from first scroll to 'Request a proposal'
  • B2B tone of voice and SEO redesigned for the corporate audience

Note: this case reflects the process and design decisions I led during the project. Leapfone's current website may have gone through further iterations since.
