About

I have been a Product Designer for 6 years. I started in development and moved into product design when I realized my real interest was understanding the problem before drawing the solution.

Today I work end to end: mapping journeys, structuring flows, testing hypotheses with real users and documenting specs for handoff with engineering, always balancing user experience, technical feasibility and business outcome.

Outside of work, I like diving into pop culture and learning about different people and places. In the end, it is all user research.

Business acumen

I design with commercial outcomes in mind, not just usability, translating business rules and constraints into interfaces that still make sense to the person using them.

AI-augmented workflow

I use AI tools throughout my process, from research synthesis to prototyping and content decisions, to move faster without cutting corners on rigor.

Complex problem solving

I am comfortable designing inside rigid business rules and multi-stakeholder constraints, where the real work is translation, not decoration.
